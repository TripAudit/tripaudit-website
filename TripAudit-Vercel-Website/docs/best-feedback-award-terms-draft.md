# TourAudit Best Feedback Award — approved operating rules, pending legal review

**Founder-approved rules (3 November 2026). Not legal clearance or authorization to open entries.**

## Organizer
TechPrompt Universe, P.O. Box 3398, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560035, India, operating the TourAudit brand. Privacy and award contact: admin@touraudit.com.

## Eligibility and entry
Open to residents of India aged 18 or older. Participation is free, voluntary and does not require a purchase. One eligible entry per person. Employees of the organizer and people administering or judging the award are excluded. Complete a TourAudit itinerary review, submit genuine feedback, include the phrase **“amazon feedback”** in the comment, and opt into award participation using the separate email and explicit consent form after feedback is saved. Email is not required for ordinary feedback. Do not include personal information in feedback comments. Award opt-in includes consented sharing of the original itinerary, generated AI report and linked feedback for product improvement and award administration; remove confidential traveller information before sharing.

## Award period
Closing date: **31 December 2026, 11:59 PM IST**. Entries must be received during the configured entry window. Opening date/time must match the actual activation date and be published before opening. An extension requires updating the public conditions, database configuration and retention arrangements before the existing deadline.

## Judging and selection
Two reviewers independently assess eligible feedback using **specificity (30%)**, **actionable suggestions (40%)**, and **relevance to improving TourAudit (30%)**. Positive and negative feedback are equally eligible; sentiment is not scored. The highest-ranked eligible entry is selected by merit, not randomly. Ties are broken by higher actionable-suggestions score, then relevance, then specificity, then the earlier eligible entry. Keep internal scoring records without public disclosure of participant content.

Judging takes place **1–7 January 2027**. Notify the selected participant by email on **8 January 2027**, with **seven days** to respond. If unclaimed or ineligible, notify the next-ranked eligible participant on **16 January 2027**, again allowing seven days. If neither claims, the award remains unawarded.

## Prize and fulfilment
**One ₹1,000 digital Amazon Pay Gift Card**, delivered by email no later than **27 January 2027** after eligibility confirmation. No claim fee. Gift-card redemption, expiry and other issuer conditions apply; verify the applicable current [issuer terms](https://www.pinelabs.com/terms-and-conditions/prepaid-instruments/branded-gift-cards/amazon-pay) before release. Amazon is not the organizer or sponsor. No public announcement of the selected person's identity or feedback content.

## Privacy and withdrawal
With explicit opt-in, TourAudit stores the participant's email, original itinerary, generated AI review, linked feedback reference and consent metadata in restricted award storage. Information is used for evaluating feedback, improving TourAudit, administering the award and contacting the selected participant, not marketing subscriptions. Ordinary feedback remains independent. Participants can withdraw their award entry using their active review session or the private withdrawal code; this deletes associated award contact and shared content from active storage without deleting ordinary feedback.

**Manual deletion of award-entry data from active storage by 31 January 2027**, subject to any legally required minimal retention. Backup retention may differ. Privacy enquiries: **admin@touraudit.com**. No public disclosure of participant identities or submitted content.
