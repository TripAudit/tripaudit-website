# TourAudit Best Feedback Award — India-only terms draft

**For founder and qualified Indian legal review only. Not open for entries.**
Prepared 2 November 2026. This document is not a public terms page, legal clearance
or authority to activate the award. Bracketed facts and proposed conditions below
must be approved and completed before publication. Proposed ages, entry limits,
times and administrative processes are not established campaign facts.

## Proposed terms

1. **Organizer.** [LEGAL NAME], [REGISTERED/CORRESPONDENCE ADDRESS], operating
   TourAudit. Campaign enquiries: [CONTACT EMAIL].
2. **Eligibility.** Residents of India [PROPOSED: aged 18 or over]. [PROPOSED:
   organizer and employees excluded; one eligible entry per person.] Participation
   is free and optional. No purchase or entry fee is required. Confirm eligibility
   restrictions and any territorial exclusions with counsel before launch.
3. **Period.** Opens [START DATE/TIME, IST]; closes 31 December 2026 at
   [PROPOSED: 23:59 IST; FOUNDER TO CONFIRM]. Both qualifying feedback and entry
   details must arrive within the final period. [CONFIRM whether earlier feedback
   qualifies; no retrospective eligibility is assumed.]
4. **Entry.** Complete a TourAudit itinerary review, submit genuine feedback about
   that experience, and include the phrase “amazon feedback” in the comment.
   [PROPOSED ENTRY PROCESS: opt into the award and supply a valid email through
   the separate verified application flow, LOCATION/PROCESS TO CONFIRM.]
   Email is optional for ordinary feedback; the proposed award flow requires a
   contact email for participation and recipient contact. No email is collected
   on the marketing website. Do not include email or confidential traveller
   information in feedback comments. [CONFIRM duplicate-entry handling.]
5. **Merit judging.** The TourAudit team will assess eligible feedback for its
   **usefulness, actionable suggestions and relevance to improving TourAudit**.
   Positive and negative feedback have equal eligibility; praise is not required
   and sentiment is not a scoring criterion. One highest-scoring eligible entry
   will receive the award, subject to approved verification and a non-random
   tie-break. Before launch, publish [SCORING ANCHORS/WEIGHTS], [JUDGING DATE],
   [PANEL/CONFLICT MANAGEMENT] and [NON-RANDOM TIE-BREAK]. Do not substitute
   unspecified discretion for the published criteria or imply chance selection.
6. **Award.** One Amazon India voucher, fixed face value ₹[EXACT DENOMINATION TO
   CONFIRM]. The internal budget is approximately US$10; this is not a finalized
   INR entitlement. Final public terms must state the exact INR amount, voucher
   type, issuer, redemption restrictions and validity. [CONFIRM cash/substitution
   policy and fulfilment costs/tax handling.] Amazon is not the organizer.
   [VERIFY issuer-approved branding and any non-sponsorship disclaimer.]
7. **Notification and fulfilment.** Selected entrant contacted by email by [DATE],
   with [CLAIM PERIOD] to respond. Verify eligibility using only necessary data
   through [SAFE PROCESS]. Deliver the voucher by [METHOD/DEADLINE]. If ineligible
   or unclaimed, follow [APPROVED NEXT-HIGHEST-SCORE PROCESS], not a chance-based
   fallback. Result communication: [PRIVACY-PRESERVING METHOD/DATE].
8. **Privacy.** [LEGAL NAME] uses entry email and necessary eligibility details
   only to administer, judge, contact and fulfil the award. Authorized access:
   [ROLES/PROCESSORS]. Retention and deletion: [DEFINED PERIOD/PROCESS], with any
   documented legal recordkeeping exception. Privacy enquiries and withdrawal:
   [CONTACT]; privacy notice: [VERIFIED LINK]. Participation is not marketing
   subscription. Ordinary feedback remains available without award entry.
   No feedback, email or personal information is publicly disclosed by this
   campaign without a separately approved lawful process. [CONFIRM withdrawal's
   effect on award entry versus ordinary feedback.]
9. **Fair administration.** [PROPOSED, SUBJECT TO APPROVAL:] exclude demonstrable
   ineligibility, duplication, impersonation, plagiarism or deliberate manipulation
   under published criteria. Never exclude honest criticism merely because it is
   negative. Keep judging records and reasons. Approve a complaints/correction
   process and only necessary exceptional cancellation provisions; do not assume
   an unrestricted right to change terms after participation.

## Activation blockers and review questions

- Confirm organizer, territory/age restrictions, dates/timezone, judging rubric,
  panel, deterministic merit tie-break, exact INR denomination, issuer product,
  contact/claim/fulfilment process and privacy notice before publishing final terms.
- **Merit selection is not automatic legal clearance.** Indian counsel should
  assess Consumer Protection Act 2019 section 2(47)(iii)(b), Consumer Protection
  (General) Rules 2020 rule 4 and applicable state requirements. Do not infer
  legality from free entry, low prize value or the change of selection method.
- Review data-protection obligations applying on the actual launch/collection date
  and to data retained later. DPDP implementation is phased; do not state that all
  core duties are already effective during 2026. Confirm notice/consent, access,
  deletion, complaint handling and applicable transitional/current requirements.
- Separate application work is required for optional contact collection and explicit
  consent. Current UI/API do not support email. See the separate readiness document.
  Do not collect email in free-text comments as a workaround.
- Publish approved final terms at a verified accessible URL, update the banner's
  terms reference, complete visual and entry-flow QA, and obtain explicit founder
  release authorization. No activation is part of this draft PR.
